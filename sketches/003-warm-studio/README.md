# Sketch: Warm Studio

## Variant: Warm Studio

### Design stance
A crafted object rather than a screen: warm stock, a terracotta accent, and depth
made from **hard offset shadows** (stacked paper) instead of blur — the opposite
mechanism to the glass direction.

### Key choices
- **Graded depth.** The first pass put the same heavy offset shadow on the prompt,
  the selected answer, the feedback note and the CTA, so nothing was the peak.
  Now: 2px for options, 4px for the prompt card and CTA, 5px plus a tinted stock
  for the feedback note, which is the moment that should stand out.
- **Solid, not dashed.** The feedback note was dashed at first and read as a
  wireframe placeholder sitting next to a solid shadow. It is now a solid card on
  sage stock, a visibly different material from the cream cards.
- **Fraunces** for display — a warmer, slightly quirkier serif than Newsreader —
  with a highlighter mark behind the target word.
- **Paper grain** as a tiny inline SVG, so the surface is not perfectly smooth.

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** warmth and approachability without tipping into childish. This is
  the friendliest of the three and the most likely to make a nervous beginner
  feel capable. Cheap to render — no backdrop filters.
- **Weak at:** the offset-shadow language is a strong commitment; applied too
  widely it becomes a costume (which is exactly what the first pass did). It also
  shares its palette neighbourhood with several well-known AI brands, so it is
  the least ownable of the three.

### Best for
- A consumer audience, a family/education framing, or anyone who bounced off the
  current build for feeling cold rather than for feeling ugly.
