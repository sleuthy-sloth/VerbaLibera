# VerbaLibera v0.2.0

- Adds a visible course path with lesson status, prerequisites, progress, review access, and a clear next lesson.
- Expands Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, and German foundations through four lessons and a second unit.
- Adds three roughly ten-minute introductory listening lessons, with transcripts, scripts, provenance, and an authoring utility.
- Adds downloadable MP3 links for listening tracks and improves service-worker handling of audio range responses.

The new Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, and German long-audio tracks are preview material pending native-speaker editorial review.
